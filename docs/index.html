About

What is Commutas

Commutas is a full-stack campus transit platform built for the students and transport staff of COMSATS University Islamabad, Abbottabad Campus. It digitizes the entire bus transport experience — from the moment a student checks a schedule to the moment they tap their phone and board.

Students can load money into a digital wallet through an online payment gateway, track active university buses on a live GPS-powered map, check remaining seat capacity in real time, and pay their fare contactlessly by holding their phone near the driver's device using NFC. Drivers manage their full trip lifecycle from within the same application, collecting fares, broadcasting their location, and handling departure sessions.

The platform also includes a web-based administrative dashboard where transport managers can configure routes, assign vehicles, manage departure schedules, and oversee the entire bus network from a single interface.

Capabilities

What it offers

Every component of campus transit, unified into one cohesive platform.

NFC Contactless Payments

Pay your fare by tapping your phone against the driver's device. Uses Android Host Card Emulation to turn your smartphone into a contactless transit card.

QR Code Fallback

Devices without NFC hardware are fully supported through a dynamic QR code system. The driver scans the code and the fare is processed identically.

Live Bus Tracking

See every active bus on a real-time interactive map with GPS positioning, estimated arrival times, route polylines, and live seat occupancy indicators.

Digital Transit Wallet

Top up your wallet through an integrated payment gateway. View your full transaction history including credits, fare deductions, and pending payments.

Portal Authentication

Sign up with your COMSATS portal credentials. Your student identity is verified automatically. Portal passwords are never stored by the application.

Admin Web Dashboard

Transport administrators manage routes, departure time slots, vehicle registrations, and driver assignments from a centralized web portal.

Getting Started

How to install

Three steps. Under a minute.

Download the APK

Tap the download button at the top of this page. The latest signed build will be downloaded from the releases page.
